The Xbox 360

The Xbox 360, released in 2005 by Microsoft, was a major upgrade from the original Xbox. It introduced high-definition graphics and became the first console to support Xbox Live in a more advanced way, offering downloadable content, digital media, and online multiplayer gaming. The Xbox 360 also featured a large library of popular games, including Halo 3, Gears of War, and The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion, which helped it become one of the best-selling consoles of its time. Additionally, the console was known for its sleek design, wireless controllers, and the Kinect, a motion-sensing device that allowed players to control games without a controller. Despite facing hardware issues early on, the Xbox 360 helped solidify Microsoft’s position in the gaming industry. It played a key role in the growth of online gaming and digital media, and its success led to the creation of the Xbox One in 2013.
